Traffic Light Controller - Experimental Results
================================================

Setup:
- Clock: 100MHz
- MIN_GREEN: 3s | MAX_GREEN: 10s | SWITCH_THRESHOLD: 2
- Measurement: worst-case wait time (clock cycles and seconds) for a car on the red side to get green
- Each experiment run once with AI controller, once with fixed-cycle (10s green / 3s yellow)
- Scenarios use the worst possible time measurement(eg. red on your side even if you are the only one) - Therefore all of the fixed wait is always 30s because that is the duration of red signal

Lane groupings: Direction 1 = Lane1 + Lane3 | Direction 2 = Lane2 + Lane4

Average % difference over all 10 scenarios:
(Summation of all reduction percentages)/(# of reduction percentages)
(90 + 86.67 + 66.67 + 66.67 + 66.67 + 66.67 + 90 + 90 + 90 + 66.67 + 66.67)/ 11 ~ 76.97%

Therefore after conducting all experiments, the average reduction in wait time for the most disadvantaged car in the intersection if in both scenarios they start when the lights just turned red is:
~ 76.97% reduction in wait time.

Keep in mind this is the absolute worst case scenarios, to gget a more general sense multiple permutations of each test must be done at different time intervals.
To simply showcase the immediate effect of the system this situation was derived for only testing purposes.
