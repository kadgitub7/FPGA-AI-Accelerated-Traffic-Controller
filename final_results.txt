Traffic Light Controller - Experimental Results
================================================

Setup:
- Clock: 100MHz
- MIN_GREEN: 3s | MAX_GREEN: 10s | SWITCH_THRESHOLD: 2
- Measurement: worst-case wait time (clock cycles and seconds) for a car on the red side to get green
- Each experiment run once with AI controller, once with fixed-cycle (10s green / 3s yellow)

Lane groupings: Direction 1 = Lane1 + Lane3 | Direction 2 = Lane2 + Lane4
